## gdx-exodus (no license published)

py-exodus began as a port of Paul Antinori's LibGDX Java implementation,
[pantinor/gdx-exodus](https://github.com/pantinor/gdx-exodus). That project
publishes no license. The following still derive from it and are being
replaced over time with LairWare-derived equivalents:

- sound effects and music (`assets/sound/`)
- XML game data (`assets/xml/`: maps, vendors, creatures, weapons, armors,
  sound, tileset)
- maps and roster (`assets/maps/`, `assets/data/`), the start-screen art
  (`assets/graphics/demon.png`, `splash_border.png`) and parts of the code's
  structure.

Rights to that material remain with its author.
