Is STR 15 right for Cleric and Wizard, given DEX 5 rarely hits?

I put real numbers on it. A caster's swings are filler — the mana pool empties, then they swing — so expected damage per swing is hit‑rate × mean damage at each possible allocation. With the Dagger (all a Wizard may wield) at the 50-point budget's options:

Wizard allocation (rest to pools)hit%dmg/swing
STR 15 / DEX 5 (yours)51.6%8.3
STR 5 / DEX 1555.5%4.7
STR 10 / DEX 10 / WIS 553.5%6.4

STR 15 wins by ~77% more filler damage — the +3.9‑point hit bonus cannot pay back the damage lost, and the cleric's Mace shows the same shape (9.4 vs 6.0). DEX 15 would raise trap evade only 30%→40%. And the caster's real damage is spells, whose scale lives in the pools. So: STR 15 on both casters is optimal; a point of INT/WIS is worth more than anything else you could buy during the game.

Gear: best non-exotic, and the Exotic truth

Class gates come from the same constraint tables Java uses, enforced on ready/wear in the port. Best non-exotic per class:

The Exotic answer: Exotic is not merely the key that opens the castle — it is simply the best gear in the game for every class. Exotic Weapon does 4–49 (beats the Sword +4's 46, and the Wizard/Druid/Alchemist can finally swing it, 4–49 vs their Dagger's 4–7). Exotic Armour's 224 defense beats Plate +2's 208 and has no class restrictions — your Wizard and Druid can wear what no shop could sell them. It costs nothing: dig sites at (33,3) and (19,44) stack it up to 9 per member. Castle Exodus only makes mandatory what was already true: the moment you dig it, the Exotics are the best equipment anyone in the party can use.

Inside the castle the mechanics harden against everything else: monsters auto-hit any member not wearing Exotic Armour (a cloth-wearer goes from ~47% hit to 100%), and only the Exotic Weapon may be equipped for attacks. Outside the castle your +4/Plate+2 gear still works normally — Exotic merely outclasses it on numbers.

The arc, in order

  • Create per above; buy Mace/Bow×2/Sword + Leathers/Chain/Plate-when-legal (~800g), a horse, torches.
  • Level at Lord British every 100 exp (full heal each level). Mark of Kings from dungeon floors I–II, before level 6 — it gates advancement past 5, and touching a mark costs 50 HP, so arrive near-full.
  • Bank chests (+38g/battle mean) and thief steals. Shrine priority: Thief DEX 25→40 (1,500g, permanent auto-hit) → Cleric WIS ≥35 (cures) then ≥50 (SANCTU MANI) → Wizard INT ≥50 (DECORP) → Fighter STR toward 99.
  • Marks of Fire/Snake (Cave of Sol) and Force (Cave of Fool) → Ambrosia → all four Cards at the search sites, per member.
  • Dig Exotics at (33,3) and (19,44). Equip everybody in Exotic Armour; fighters swing Exotic Weapons; casters blast (DECORP, the mass-killers) and heal.
  • Castle of Death: insert the four Cards — Love, Sol, Moons, Death on their panels at y=12. A wrong panel kills the inserter.

Caveats and sources

The community's formulas (two-roll hit ≈ 50%+DEX/2; class-based trap evade with Thief 128/255; damage 4+3×rank+⌈STR/2⌉+rand(STR)) come from the original's disassembly. Your port's implemented shapes differ somewhat, and this page computes with the implemented ones deliberately. Two quirks worth knowing: ZXKuqyb carried a ported Java bug until 2026-11-15 (now a fair 75%-per-monster kill per LairWare's Apple‑II-derived code); mass-kill spells use a per-battle 50% gate, so one "FAILED" at the wrong encounter is the mechanic, not a defect.
